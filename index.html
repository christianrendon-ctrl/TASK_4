My Childhood

Growing up in a lively community surrounded by nature and coastal scenery, my early childhood was filled with curiosity, active games with friends, and discovering how things work. I always loved sketching ideas, exploring the outdoors, and learning something new every single day.

During my teenage years, I developed an intense fascination with computers, digital design, and programming languages. Alongside sports and community activities, I spent countless hours experimenting with graphic layouts and web design, building a solid foundation for creative digital craft.

My Adult Life

As an adult at 26, I have embraced a career as a freelance web designer and digital creative in Cagayan de Oro. I enjoy crafting modern, accessible digital experiences, solving visual challenges, and continuously exploring emerging web technologies to create meaningful work.

My Favorites

Music is an essential companion in my creative workflow as a designer. I deeply enjoy listening to indie, acoustic, and ambient music artists whose soulful vocals, mellow melodies, and expressive arrangements spark imagination. Listening to calming acoustic tracks keeps me motivated during long design sessions and helps me unwind after a productive day of work.

One of my favorite foods is freshly prepared artisanal burgers and stone-baked pizza. I appreciate meals crafted with fresh ingredients, balanced seasonings, and satisfying flavors that make every bite enjoyable. It is the ultimate comfort meal that I always look forward to enjoying with family and friends after completing an exciting creative milestone.

The cool highland mountains of Dahilayan and Bukidnon, located just a short ride from Cagayan de Oro, are among my absolute favorite places. The panoramic mountain views, pine trees, and fresh mountain air provide a peaceful retreat away from urban noise. Visiting scenic nature spots recharges my creative energy and inspires organic harmony in my digital designs.
